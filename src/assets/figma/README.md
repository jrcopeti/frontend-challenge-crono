## Substituted with agreement

Sidebar nav icons come from Lucide: the design's glyphs could not be traced to a published
icon set, and substituting was agreed rather than assumed. Each was chosen by enlarging the
export and matching the shape — Inbox is a flagged mailbox, not a tray; Deals is a stack of
discs, not sheets.
